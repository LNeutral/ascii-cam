WHAT IS ASCII-CAM?

ASCII-CAM converts your live camera feed into real-time ASCII art — mapping each pixel's brightness to a character from a dense ramp, from light spaces to heavy symbols. No video is stored or sent anywhere; everything runs locally in your browser.

MEDIAPIPE SELFIE SEGMENTATION

The CUTOUT feature is powered by Google's @mediapipe/selfie_segmentation — a lightweight on-device ML model that isolates a person from the background in real time. It runs entirely in the browser via WebAssembly and WebGL; no data leaves your device.
